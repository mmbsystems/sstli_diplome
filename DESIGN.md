# SSTLI Emerald Design System

## Direction

Modern institutional product UI with premium restraint, generous spacing, clean educational content, and familiar controls.

## Color

- Primary: `#0A9B4A`
- Primary dark: `#08753A`
- Primary soft: `#EAF7EF`
- Accent/error: `#B21F24`
- Text: `#111111`, secondary `#667085`, muted `#98A2B3`
- Background: `#F8FAF9`, surface `#FFFFFF`, border `#E5EAE7`

## Typography

Use Cairo with Tahoma and sans-serif fallbacks. Fixed product-oriented type scale, 16px minimum body copy, balanced headings, readable line length.

## Shape and elevation

Radii: 10px small, 16px medium, 20px cards, 22px large, 24px hero. Shadows remain subtle: `0 8px 30px rgba(16, 24, 40, 0.06)`.

## Components and interaction

Cards are reserved for selectable categories, study modes, and comparable programs. Buttons and selectors expose hover, focus-visible, active, disabled, error, and selected states. Motion is limited to 4–8px state transitions under 250ms and respects reduced motion.

## Layout

Mobile-first, one-column mobile, two-column tablet, three-column desktop, maximum content width 1240px. Use a compact progress trail, a mobile filters sheet, and a sticky mobile detail CTA where useful.
