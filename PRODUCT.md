# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Prospective Arabic-speaking SSTLI students comparing diplomas, qualifying courses, and development courses by study mode, city, branch, gender availability, price, and duration.

## Product Purpose

Help a student reach the correct currently available program and offering through a short RTL wizard, understand verified program details, and calculate diploma installments without exposing uncertain data as fact.

## Brand Personality

Institutional, trustworthy, calm, modern, and educational.

## Anti-references

No crowded red-and-green styling, random stock imagery, invented course data, long form-like onboarding, decorative glassmorphism, or excessive animation.

## Design Principles

1. Ask only for choices needed to narrow the catalog.
2. Keep program content separate from branch-specific commercial offerings.
3. Make verified details clear and uncertain details explicitly unavailable.
4. Put mobile readability and Arabic RTL behavior first.
5. Use restrained visual hierarchy to build trust.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Arabic language and RTL direction throughout, keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic labels, useful alt text, 44px touch targets, good contrast, and reduced-motion support.
